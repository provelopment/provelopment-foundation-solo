### 4.1 Safe bounded readiness handoff

Modify the **existing** Linux `session-parent` supervision path. Before the parent invokes the existing read-only `observe_process_identity_v1(Some(handle_pid))`, it must have actually received, assembled and **strictly validated a complete fixed child-readiness line** from its owned child's stdout pipe. `Child::id()` may be obtained earlier, but must not by itself grant native query authority. The child remains the **existing** fixed `session-child`, whose old behavior is frozen: it verifies all four limits and `pid == pgid == sid`, flushes exactly one `SOLO-MEDIA-HOST-SESSION-V1 ...\n` readiness record, sleeps `SESSION_CHILD_SLEEP_MS=4_000` milliseconds, then exits normally with 0. The parent never trusts child text as proof of its own observation or reap. It must still independently query **once** and validate `pid`, `pgid`, `sid`, `observed_pgid`, `observed_sid`.

The current general stdout `read_bounded` reader drains to **EOF**; joining/awaiting that reader cannot be a readiness barrier because the child remains alive during the four-second window. Replace or narrowly adapt the **session path only** with a safe, bounded early-completion handoff (for example a single bounded `std::sync::mpsc` message from the capped stdout reader immediately upon its first complete newline). Do **not** wait for the child's EOF before the parent query. The parent receives the readiness event **before** the group/session kernel query, parses it with the current `parse_session_child_readiness_v1` and `validate_session_child_v1` strict validators, and checks the reported positive child pid equals the exact `Child::id()` from its own handle. The parent then runs the existing two `try_wait()` liveness checks bracketing the sole read-only kernel `getpgid`/`getsid` observation. A mismatch or an already-exited child is a closed refusal; no poll/retry/second identity query.

**Readiness data and total output are bounded:** preserve `MAX_CHILD_STDOUT_BYTES=1024`, `MAX_CHILD_STDERR_BYTES=512` and the existing UTF-8/exact ASCII-decimal protocol. Never use an unbounded `read_to_end`, `read_line`, `read_until`, unlimited buffering or an unbounded queue for hostile/malformed streams. The maximum includes the entire stdout stream, not a separate allowance per phase. A newline must be complete before granting readiness. Any missing newline, extra bytes/fields, duplicate line, incorrect order, over-cap line/stream, invalid UTF-8, closed early stream, failed reader or untrusted child pid **refuses**; do not truncate it into success. A reader must continue bounded capture through EOF after its early readiness notification, so the parent's final post-reap validation rejects trailing output rather than trusting a partial early prefix. The captured early line and final full stream must agree exactly; the existing public `media_host_session=live_attested ...\n` acceptance line must remain unchanged in key names, order and count.

Use a bounded one-shot readiness handoff (a one-message bounded channel or equivalent safe synchronization). Do not spin or busy-loop on pipes, invent arbitrary commands, or use global state. The child's stdout reader must not wait for the main thread to `join()` before sending readiness. The main thread must not join either reader while the child is still alive. On timeout/refusal it may kill and **reap only its directly owned `Child` handle**, never a negative pid, process group, foreign process or descendant. Join all created readers **after** terminating/reaping the leader; report no acceptance on reader overflow, error, panic or failed join. The child is trusted and descendant-free, so this order does not establish safety when descendants inherit pipe handles.
