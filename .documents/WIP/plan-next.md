# Foundation Solo — plan-next (Next Candidate)

## R00.D — Content and publication contract

**Objective:** Prepare an Owner-reviewable physical content and publication declaration contract for Solo's one-Entity EFS graph, JSON/Markdown authoring, visibility, source integrity and safe asset resolution, implementing — not revising — accepted R00.A identity/publication, R00.B address/locale and R00.C property/location/graphic logical rules.

**Prerequisites and readiness:** R00.A, R00.B and R00.C logical architectural packages are accepted and Owner-authorized for administrative closure. The existing canonical Product Definition and Architecture Charter remain binding. R00.D is pending in Stage 00. This NEXT candidate is decisions-only and non-executable.

**Proposed scope:** Compare precise, versioned publication manifest and authoring schema options; define JSON/Markdown source declarations, precedence and safe bounded Page sections; encode the accepted Page `visibilityDefault`/representation `visibility` contract; define link safety/classification, source path and asset-reference authority, and physical property/location/graphic declaration representation consistent with C01–C10. Define positive and refusal cases for missing/undeclared sources, duplicate claims, path ambiguity, unlisted publication, malformed Markdown, private projections and forbidden executable content. Do not change accepted identity, locale, routing, property or graphic semantics.

**Proposed deliverables:** A bounded R00.D decision proposal with credible options and recommendations; explicit Owner decisions on blocking physical content/publication rules; precise proposed amendments to the existing canonical Product/Architecture authorities; a conceptual validation/refusal matrix. Keep implementation stack, dependency/toolchain and runtime adapters deferred to R00.E and later stages.

**Verifiable exit conditions:** A single coherent and versioned declaration/schema contract can represent accepted R00.A–R00.C semantics; published graph and safe source resolution are fail-closed; draft versus published, public versus unlisted, Page/representation visibility, asset ownership and JSON/Markdown parity are unambiguous and testable. Stage 00 remains open until R00.E–R00.G and the Stage 00 exit gate are accepted.

This NEXT candidate is **non-executable**. No R00.D decisions, edits or implementation are authorized by its presence.
